# Governance Decay: How Context Compaction Silently Erases Safety Constraints

- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22528
- **Kind:** Paper
- **Date:** 2026-06
- **Relates to:** ADR-0002f (goals lens decay exemption), ADR-0002b §4

Documents compaction quietly dropping pinned safety rules from long-horizon histories, and shows explicit pinning removes violations at no utility cost. Independent experimental validation of the goals-lens value-profile override: standing intent must not ride the same decay curve as episodic content, or it silently evaporates. The pinned-rule fix is our foundational zone; the paper supplies the failure statistics we get to design against.
