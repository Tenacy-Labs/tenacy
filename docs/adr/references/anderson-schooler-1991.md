# Reflections of the Environment in Memory (Anderson & Schooler, 1991)

- **Link:** https://users.cs.northwestern.edu/~paritosh/papers/KIP/AndersonSchooler1991ReflectionsOfEnvironmentOnMemory.pdf
- **Kind:** Paper (Psychological Science)
- **Date:** 1991
- **Relates to:** ADR-0002b §2 (power-law decay)

The theoretical ancestor of our value model. Shows human memory need probabilities mirror environmental statistics — recency and frequency of past use predict future need, with power-law form — across New York Times headlines, parental speech, and email. Our μ₀ + (1+Δt)^−α is ACT-R's base-level activation (B_i = ln Σ t_j^−d) re-derived as an expected-value forecast. Thirty-five years of cognitive literature behind one term of the objective; citing it keeps us honest that the decay curve is an environmental regularity, not an invention.
